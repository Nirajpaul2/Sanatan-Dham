Account Deletion Request

We respect your control over your data. Request permanent deletion of your Sanatan Dham account and associated information.

Account Deletion Guidelines

If you wish to delete your Sanatan Dham mobile application account, you can initiate a deletion request below or directly inside the app under Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

What happens when your account is deleted:

  • Personal Profile: Your registered phone number, email address, user profile name, and authentication records are permanently erased from our primary database.
  • Saved Favorites & History: Your saved Aartis, Bhajans, favorite wallpaper list, ringtone downloads, and listening history will be deleted.
  • Device Tokens: Your Firebase Push Notification device ID will be deactivated, stopping all daily devotional push notifications.
  • VIP Subscriptions: Active Razorpay AutoPay mandates should be cancelled prior to account deletion to avoid unexpected bank debit renewals.

Information retained for legal compliance:

In accordance with statutory financial regulations in India, historical transaction records (Razorpay payment receipts, GST invoices, and mandate authorization timestamps) are retained for legal and auditing purposes as required by law.
